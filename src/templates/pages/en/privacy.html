Privacy

This policy explains how Hub-i3 handles personal data and usage metrics in compliance with the Brazilian LGPD (Law No. 13,709/2018).

Data collected

For authenticated users, account data may be processed (name, email, profile, permissions). For public browsing, technical and aggregated usage metrics may be collected.

Processing purposes

Data is used for authentication, content management, platform security, operational communication, and statistical analysis to improve service quality.

Legal bases

Processing may rely on public-policy execution, legitimate administrative interest, legal obligations, and, when applicable, user consent.

Data subject rights

Data subjects may request access, correction, and updates of their information, subject to legal and operational constraints.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies may be used to maintain sessions and preferences. Usage metrics are handled with an emphasis on aggregate data to avoid undue personal identification.
