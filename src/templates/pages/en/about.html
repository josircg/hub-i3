About Hub-i3

The Hub for Information and Inclusive Innovation (Hub-i3) is a digital platform for consulting public policies aimed at people and groups in situations of social vulnerability.

The platform organizes policies by life cycle and population group, offering text search, filters, and category-based navigation.

Public consultation is open and does not require registration. Content management and user administration are restricted to authenticated profiles.

Hub-i3 is designed for a broad audience, including:

  • Citizens looking for clear access to public policy information;
  • Researchers and civil society organizations working with social inclusion topics;
  • Public managers responsible for maintaining and monitoring the platform.

Hub-i3 objectives

Hub-i3 aims to gather and organize public policy information, facilitate discovery through search and filtering, ensure free and accessible access, support continuous content maintenance, and provide open-source usage metrics for platform governance.
